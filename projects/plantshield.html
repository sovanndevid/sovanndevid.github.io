PROJECT OVERVIEW

A Django-based university project for growers to record farm information and observe pest or disease activity. The repository provides the application models, forms, class-based views and HTML templates.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Understand the problem.

Plant-health observations become harder to follow when records are unstructured. The project models relationships between farms, individual trees, surveillance reports and different pests or diseases.

02 / THE APPROACH

Design the approach.

Django models represent growers, farms, trees, pests, affected plant parts and surveillance records. The views implement registration, farm profile editing and dashboards, including farm-specific record queries and authentication checks.
