Methodology

Six families — RSI contrarian, RSI trend pullback, EMA20 mean reversion, EMA20 momentum, candle continuation and candle reversal — across 720 parameter combinations. Fixed 60% training / 20% validation / 20% later-period test.

Signals use only completed candles, entry occurs at the next candle open, stops are assumed to execute before targets on ambiguous same-candle touches, and positions do not overlap. Each trade deducts US$0.75 per ounce in total assumed round-trip costs. This is a modelling assumption, not an Exness spread quote.

Why the high-win-rate target is dangerous

A high winning percentage can still lose money.

Strategies with tiny take-profits and wide stops can show 70–90% wins while a few losses overwhelm the small winners. Transaction costs can eliminate an otherwise thin advantage. Do not increase leverage or trade frequency just to force a better-looking win rate.
